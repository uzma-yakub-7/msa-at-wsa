Why Join the MSA?

Students can participate in MSA meetings, Islamic educational programs, community activities, prayer initiatives, and leadership opportunities.

The MSA provides a welcoming space where students can build friendships, strengthen their faith, develop leadership skills, and contribute positively to the school community.

Who Can Join?

The MSA is primarily for Muslim students at Westchester Square Academy.

Non-Muslim students who are interested in learning about Islam and Muslim culture are also welcome to participate respectfully.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to be Muslim to participate?

No. While the MSA is primarily for Muslim students, non-Muslim students interested in learning about Islam and Muslim culture are welcome to participate respectfully.

Do I need previous MSA experience?

No. Students can participate whether they are new to the MSA or have been involved in student organizations before.

How do I find out about events?

Upcoming events and announcements will be posted on the website and through the MSA's official communication channels.

Can members take leadership roles?

Leadership opportunities will be available as the MSA develops its student leadership structure. More information will be announced soon.
