Welcome

A place to gather, grow, and belong.

The MSA at Westchester Square Academy is a student-led community where students can strengthen their faith, build friendships, develop leadership skills, and contribute positively to our school community.

✦

Faith

Create opportunities for students to learn, reflect, pray, and strengthen their connection with their faith.

Leadership

What Guides Us

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Strengthen faith. Build unity.

Our mission is to strengthen faith, build unity, and promote understanding by creating a welcoming community where Muslim students can grow spiritually, develop as leaders, and make a positive impact.

Our Vision

Inspire faith. Uplift others.

Our vision is to be a strong, united, and active Muslim student community that inspires faith, uplifts others, and leaves a lasting legacy.
